User Data Deletion Instructions for Viyona Designs

Effective Date: August 2026
Website: viyonadesigns.com

1. Overview

In accordance with Meta Platform policies and general data protection standards, Viyona Designs provides users with full control over any personal data collected through our website, social applications, or customer interactions.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to remove your user data, customer records, or any associated data from our systems, you can easily submit a deletion request following these steps:

  1. Send an email to support@viyonadesigns.com or viyonadesigns@gmail.com with the subject line: "User Data Deletion Request".
  2. In your email, include:
    • Your full name
    • The email address or phone number associated with your order or inquiry
    • Your Facebook / Instagram profile handle (if applicable)

3. Processing Time & Confirmation

Upon receiving your request, our team will verify your identity and permanently delete all associated personal records from our databases within 48 to 72 hours. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been purged.
